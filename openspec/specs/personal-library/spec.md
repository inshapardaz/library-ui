# Personal Library Specification

## Purpose
User-owned collections: favorites and bookshelves.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Favorites
Users SHALL be able to favorite and unfavorite books, poetry and writings.

#### Scenario: Favorite a book
- **WHEN** a user activates the favorite button on a book
- **THEN** the book is added to their favorites

### Requirement: Bookshelves
Users SHALL be able to view their bookshelves and the books in each.

#### Scenario: Open a shelf
- **WHEN** a user opens a bookshelf
- **THEN** its books are listed
